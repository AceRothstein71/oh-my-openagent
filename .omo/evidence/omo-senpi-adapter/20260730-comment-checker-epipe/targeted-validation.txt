ELF ABI inspection
-------------------
Installed package:
  @code-yeongyu/comment-checker@0.8.0

Linux x64 identity:
  ELF 64-bit LSB PIE executable, x86-64, dynamically linked,
  interpreter /lib64/ld-linux-x86-64.so.2, stripped.

Highest imported version found in both Linux artifacts:
  linux-x64: GLIBC_2.34
  linux-arm64: GLIBC_2.34

Representative undefined imports from linux-x64:
  GLIBC_2.32 pthread_getattr_np
  GLIBC_2.33 fstat64
  GLIBC_2.33 stat64
  GLIBC_2.34 __libc_start_main
  GLIBC_2.34 pthread_attr_getstack
  GLIBC_2.34 pthread_key_create

Conclusion:
  Ubuntu 20.04 is one confirmed glibc 2.31 reproduction. Debian 11 and other
  Linux systems below the binary's glibc 2.34 requirement are also ABI-risk
  environments. The EPIPE containment is intentionally platform-independent
  because an early child exit can have causes other than glibc.
